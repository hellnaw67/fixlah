Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 July 2026 · Governed by Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)

FixLah connects you with independent service professionals in the Klang Valley. This notice explains, in plain language, what personal data we collect when you request quotes, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in the request form: the service you need, your area or postcode, your name, an optional phone number, a description of the job, and any photos you choose to attach.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (only if analytics is enabled): aggregate page views and how many requests are submitted. This is cookieless and does not identify you.

We do not collect payment details, IC numbers, passwords, or any special-category data through this site.

How your request reaches us

When you submit the form, your device opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message that you send to us. Your information travels to us through WhatsApp, whose own privacy terms then apply. We do not store your data on this website — there is no database behind it.

Why we use it

  • To understand your job and pass the relevant details to a small number of vetted independent pros who can quote for it.
  • To reply to you, arrange a booking, and follow up on how the job went.
  • To improve our service using anonymous, aggregate statistics only.

Who we share it with

Only the independent professionals we select to quote on your specific job, and only the details they need to quote (service, area, and description). We never sell your data or share it for third-party marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your request details for as long as needed to arrange the job and provide any follow-up or warranty support, then we remove personal identifiers we no longer need. Anonymous statistics may be kept indefinitely.

Your rights under the PDPA

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data you have shared with us, and you may withdraw consent at any time. To do so, message us on the same WhatsApp number you used to send your request.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us on WhatsApp via the quote request page, or email hello@fixlah.my.
