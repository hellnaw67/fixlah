Pro questions

What tradespeople ask us.

Do you take a cut of what I charge customers?
No. You quote each job and agree the price directly with the customer, and you keep all of it. FixLah charges a flat monthly listing fee to be on the network and get matched — that's it. (If we ever add optional paid extras like featured placement, they'll always be clearly opt-in.)
When do I actually start paying?
Only after we verify you and your profile goes live. Applying is free. Founding members then get 90 days free before their first payment, and keep 50% off for life.
How do jobs reach me?
When a customer near you posts a job in your trade, we send you the details on WhatsApp. You choose which jobs to quote — there's no obligation to take every one.
What do you check before I'm listed?
We verify your identity (IC), your trade and coverage areas, and take a look at examples of your past work. Keeping the network genuinely vetted is what makes customers trust it — and what protects your reputation.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel whenever you like — your listing simply comes down at the end of the paid period.
