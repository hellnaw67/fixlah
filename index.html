Good to know

Questions people ask first.

How much does it cost to get quotes?
Getting quotes is completely free. You only ever pay the contractor you choose, at the price the two of you agree before any work begins. We make our money from the pros, never from surprise fees on you.
How do I know the contractors are trustworthy?
Every pro on the network is ID-verified and rated by past customers. You see their rating, number of jobs and reviews before you book — and anyone who doesn’t keep to our standards is removed.
Do I get any warranty on the work?
Many jobs come with a workmanship warranty from the contractor. Where one applies, we’ll show you the terms before you book so there are no surprises.
Which areas do you cover right now?
We’re starting in the Klang Valley (KL, PJ, Subang, Shah Alam and nearby) and adding new areas as we grow. Send your request anyway — if we can’t help yet, we’ll tell you honestly.
How fast will I hear back?
Most requests get their first quotes back the same day, often within the hour during working hours. Urgent jobs like a breakdown or a leak get prioritised.
